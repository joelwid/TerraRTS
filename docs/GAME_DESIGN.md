# Game design v0.1
## Confirmed scope
Single-player slow real-time economic RTS, one fixed continuous landmass, flat 2D isometric grid, static visuals. Rocket launch sole victory. No workers, citizen tiers, recurring population or unit consumption, roads, logistics, electricity, fuel, depletion or rival colonies. Population is purchased via residences and unlocks efficient upgrades. Horizontal expansion expensive, geological information and terraforming cheap. Hostile local aliens attack nearby buildings/units; destroying nests grants one-time rations, iron or silicon rewards. Peaceful victory must remain possible. Towers favor compact defense.
## World
Proposed 96x96 square logical grid, 64x32 diamond tiles, camera pan/zoom, no rotation. Tile fields: terrain, claimed, scanned, fertile, underground resource, deposit ID, occupancy, buildable, walkable. Dark brown unclaimed, light brown claimed; known resource cracks blue water, silver iron, light blue silicon. Fertile green with static vegetation, retaining known cracks. Claimed implies scanned. Scanning and fertility permanent. Scanned empty unclaimed land distinguishable via tooltip/overlay. No shimmer or sprite animation.
All underground deposits exactly 2x2, uniform, infinite, unique ID. Iron Mine, Water Tower and Silicon Mine each exactly 2x2, must cover all four tiles of one matching deposit, claimed and unoccupied. No partial coverage. Proposed agriculture requires fully fertile footprint. All other buildings require claimed buildable unoccupied footprint. Starting Hub exception.
## Infrastructure defaults
Hub 3x3 radius 8, Terraformer 1x1 radius 5, Geo Scanner 1x1 radius 20. Radius measured in logical Euclidean tile units from building center. Hub claims and scans; overlapping claims survive removal of one Hub. Terraformer fertilizes claimed tiles once on completion; scanner scans even outside claims once on completion. Lost claim does not erase knowledge/fertility. Proposed unclaimed buildings inactive; use connectivity to original Hub to prevent self-supporting disconnected expansion. Original Hub destruction proposed defeat; cannot voluntarily demolish original Hub.
## Map
Handcraft starting iron/water and fertile-capable land; safe western expansion iron/water, northern silicon/iron; eastern small nest guarding extra iron, southern larger nest guarding silicon. Add enough peaceful deposits for low-pop victory. Validate map coordinates, nonoverlap and navigable routes. No procedural generator or editor.
## UX
Resource bar, gross/actual/net production, population and next milestone, build menu, selection details, upgrade preview, rocket requirements from start, minimap, territory/fertility/scanning/threat overlays, notifications, clock, pause/1x/2x. WASD/arrows pan, wheel zoom, left select/place, drag unit selection, right move/attack, A attack-move, Esc cancel, Space pause. Static construction/upgrade progress bars, distinct level sprites, simple hit indicators. Main menu, restart, results, no saves. Smooth unit/camera movement is gameplay, not sprite animation.
